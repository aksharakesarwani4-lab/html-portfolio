About Me

I’m especially interested in psychology, with a growing fascination for areas like forensic psychology, trauma, and the relationship between human behavior and technology. At the same time, I enjoy exploring the world of AI, technology, and creative digital work. I like learning how things work, experimenting with new ideas, and finding ways to bring different fields together.

Beyond academics, I enjoy exploring creative things such as content writing, design, editing, coding, and learning new skills. I’m someone who tends to get curious about almost everything, from the mysteries of the human mind to strange paradoxes and mythical ideas. I believe learning isn’t limited to a classroom. Every new skill, mistake, project, and random rabbit hole teaches you something.

My goal is to keep exploring, keep creating, and eventually build a career where psychology and technology can work together to create something meaningful. This portfolio is a small collection of what I’m learning, creating, and working toward.
